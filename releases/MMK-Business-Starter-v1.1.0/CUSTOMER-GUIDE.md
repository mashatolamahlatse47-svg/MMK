# MMK BUSINESS STARTER SYSTEM
## Customer Guide — Version 1.1.0

MMK Business Starter is a lightweight browser-based business management tool for small and growing businesses.

### Products
Add products with their name, category, selling price, cost price and stock quantity.

### Making a Sale
Open Sales, select a product, enter quantity and payment details, then review the automatic calculations before recording the sale.

The Sales calculator shows:
- Sales Total
- Cost Total
- Total Profit
- Stock Remaining
- Amount Owed

Stock is automatically reduced after a successful sale.

### Adding Products From Sales
Use Add Product when the product you need is not yet available.

### Reports
Reports provide sales, costs, profit, cash received, outstanding debt, customers, products, stock and sales history.

### Running the Product
python -m http.server 8080 --directory web

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080 in your browser.
