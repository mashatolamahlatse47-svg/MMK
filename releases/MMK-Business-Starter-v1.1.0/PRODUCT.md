# MMK BUSINESS STARTER — BUILT PRODUCT DETAILS

## Version 1.1.0

### Sales Improvements

- Sales now displays the available products.
- Products can be selected directly from the Sales screen.
- A new Add Product button allows a product to be added when needed.
- Sales automatically calculates the selling total.
- Sales automatically calculates the total cost.
- Sales automatically calculates gross profit.
- Sales automatically calculates stock remaining before the sale is recorded.
- Existing stock is reduced automatically after a successful sale.

MMK Business Starter is a reusable digital business management product created by MMK for small and growing businesses.

The product we built includes:

### Business Information
- Business name
- Owner name
- Phone / WhatsApp
- Email
- Website
- Business category

### Products
- Product name
- Category
- Selling price
- Cost price
- Stock quantity
- Automatic profit per unit

### Customers
- Customer name
- Money owed
- Debt history
- Debt date
- Notes

### Sales
The sales system supports:
- Cash
- Credit
- Partial Payment

It automatically calculates:
- Sales total
- Cost total
- Profit
- Amount owed

Credit and partial-payment sales update customer debt information, while completed sales automatically reduce product stock.

### Reports
The Reports section provides a history-based view of:
- Total sales
- Total cost
- Total profit
- Cash received
- Outstanding debt
- Customers
- Products
- Units sold
- Current stock
- Sales history
- Customer debt history
- Product and stock information
