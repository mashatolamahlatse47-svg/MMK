# MMK Receipt Generator — Customer Guide

## Quick Start

Open the MMK Receipt Generator in your browser.

### 1. Business Details

Enter your:

- Business name
- Phone or WhatsApp number

### 2. Receipt Details

Enter:

- Receipt number
- Date
- Customer name

### 3. Add Items

For each product or service enter:

- Item name
- Quantity
- Price

Use **+ Add Item** when another item is needed.

Use **Remove** to delete an item.

### 4. Payment

Select the payment method:

- Cash
- EFT
- Card
- Mobile Payment
- Other

You can also enter a payment reference.

### 5. Discount

Enter the discount amount if one applies.

### 6. Generate

Select:

**Generate Receipt**

Check the calculated total.

### 7. Print or Save

Select:

**Print / Save PDF**

Your phone or computer can then use its printing/PDF option.

## Important

Always check:

- Customer name
- Items
- Quantities
- Prices
- Discount
- Final total
- Payment reference

before giving the receipt to the customer.
