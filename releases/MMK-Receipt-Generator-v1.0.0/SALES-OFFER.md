# MMK Receipt Generator — Sales Offer

## Simple receipts for small businesses

MMK Receipt Generator is a lightweight browser-based receipt tool designed for small businesses that want to create clean customer receipts from a phone or computer.

## What the customer gets

- MMK Receipt Generator application
- Customer guide
- Receipt template
- Sample receipt
- Phone/browser-friendly interface
- Automatic receipt total calculation
- Print / Save PDF function

## Suitable for

- Food sellers
- Clothing sellers
- Beauty businesses
- Service providers
- Small shops
- Home businesses
- Freelancers

## How it works

1. Open the application.
2. Enter business details.
3. Enter customer details.
4. Add products or services.
5. Enter quantities and prices.
6. Generate the receipt.
7. Print or save the receipt as PDF.

## Important

This product is a receipt-generation tool. It is not presented as tax, accounting, bookkeeping, or legal compliance software.
