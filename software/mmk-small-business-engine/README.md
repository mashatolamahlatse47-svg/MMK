# MMK Small Business Engine

Reusable business-management engine for small businesses.

## Purpose

The engine provides reusable foundations for:

- Stock management
- Sales recording
- Customer records
- Credit / "take now, pay later"
- Payments and debt tracking
- Expenses
- Profit calculations
- Business reports
- Security and audit controls

## Product Strategy

MMK Small Business Engine is the reusable core asset.

Business-specific products such as:

- Vetkoek Business
- Kota Business
- Spaza Business
- Other small-business solutions

will be customised from this engine rather than rebuilt from scratch.
