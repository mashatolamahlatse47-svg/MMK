# MMK Frontier Technology Lab

## Purpose

The MMK Frontier Technology Lab is the research and development area for emerging technologies that may create future MMK products, services, intellectual property, skills, and businesses.

## Technology Areas

- AI
- Energy
- Space
- Biotechnology
- Semiconductors
- Robotics
- Digital Assets
- Climate Technology
- New Business Models

## MMK Learning Cycle

LEARN
→ RESEARCH
→ EXPERIMENT
→ BUILD
→ TEST
→ DOCUMENT
→ COMMERCIALIZE
→ PROTECT
→ IMPROVE

## Core Principle

MMK does not chase technology trends blindly.

We investigate real problems, learn the technology, build controlled experiments, test usefulness, and only then consider commercial products.

## Founder Control

AI assists MMK.

The founder defines problems, reviews important decisions, evaluates products, and gives final approval before release.

## Safety

Experiments must remain legal, ethical, safe, and within authorized systems and data.
