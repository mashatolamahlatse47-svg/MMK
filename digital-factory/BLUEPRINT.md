# MMK DIGITAL FACTORY BLUEPRINT

## 1. PURPOSE

The MMK Digital Factory is the internal production and entrepreneurship system used by MMK to turn ideas, opportunities, skills and knowledge into legitimate digital products, services, training and commercial opportunities.

The Factory is designed to make production repeatable.

---

## 2. FIRST MACHINE

The first machine is the MMK Digital Factory Dashboard.

The dashboard is the internal control centre for Factory operations.

It must allow an authorised MMK operator to:

- create opportunities
- review opportunities
- convert suitable opportunities into products or services
- register and manage members
- assign members to legitimate production work
- track manufacturing stages
- perform quality checks
- record training generated from production
- manage customers
- track the lifecycle of Factory assets

---

## 4. OPPORTUNITY

An Opportunity represents a legitimate problem, need, idea, production possibility, customer request or business opportunity that MMK may develop.

Example categories:

- digital product
- website
- software
- automation
- digital service
- design
- training
- local business solution
- creative asset
- partnership

An Opportunity must be evaluated before becoming production work.

Minimum Opportunity information:

- Opportunity ID
- title
- description
- source
- category
- target customer/user
- expected value
- required skills
- status
- owner
- created date
- notes

---

## 5. PRODUCT

A Product is a repeatable digital or intellectual asset produced by the Factory.

Examples:

- calculator
- web application
- software package
- template
- design pack
- digital guide
- training product
- licensed digital asset

Minimum Product information:

- Product ID
- name
- version
- description
- source opportunity
- owner
- manufacturing status
- quality status
- pricing
- intellectual-property status
- release status

---

## 6. SERVICE

A Service is a repeatable activity MMK can provide to a customer or organisation.

Examples:

- website development
- digital setup
- software support
- design service
- business digitisation
- training
- maintenance

Minimum Service information:

- Service ID
- name
- description
- source opportunity
- required skills
- delivery workflow
- pricing model
- responsible member/team
- service status
- quality requirements

---

## 7. MEMBER

A Member is a person participating in the Factory ecosystem.

A member may:

- learn
- train
- manufacture
- assist with services
- build a portfolio
- participate in approved opportunities
- create products
- develop independent business capabilities

Minimum Member information:

- Member ID
- name
- skills
- training status
- portfolio
- opportunities
- assigned work
- participation status

Commercial arrangements must be agreed before paid work begins.

---

## 8. MANUFACTURING

Manufacturing is the process of turning an approved Opportunity into a usable Product or Service.

Standard stages:

1. INTAKE
2. PLAN
3. BUILD
4. CHECK
5. TEST
6. FIX
7. QUALITY REVIEW
8. PACKAGE
9. RELEASE
10. PRESERVE

Every manufacturing job should have a clear owner and status.

---

## 9. QUALITY

Nothing becomes a Factory release simply because it was built.

Quality checks should verify:

- functionality
- usability
- correctness
- documentation
- security
- intellectual-property considerations
- customer readiness
- version information
- preservation/backup status

A failed quality check returns the work to the appropriate manufacturing stage.

---

## 10. TRAINING

Production knowledge that is suitable for reuse may be converted into:

- tutorials
- SOPs
- checklists
- courses
- guides
- exercises
- internal training material

The purpose is to prevent knowledge from disappearing after one project.

---

↓

↓
DEMO
↓
READY
↓
COMMERCIAL
↓
MAINTENANCE
↓
RETIRE / ARCHIVE

---

## 12. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Factory assets must be identifiable and preserved.

Each appropriate asset should record:

- creator/contributor
- owner
- creation date
- version
- licence
- source materials
- dependencies
- commercial restrictions
- release status

No asset should be commercially released without the appropriate ownership and permission information being recorded.

---

## 13. FACTORY FEEDBACK LOOP

Every completed production cycle should generate learning.

PRODUCTION
↓
RESULT
↓
REVIEW
↓
LESSON
↓
TRAINING / DOCUMENTATION
↓
IMPROVED WORKFLOW
↓
NEXT PRODUCTION

This is how the Factory becomes more capable over time.

---

## 14. DASHBOARD PRINCIPLE

The dashboard should show the Factory as an operating system.

The main dashboard should eventually answer:

- What opportunities exist?
- What is being manufactured?
- What products exist?
- What services exist?
- Which members are active?
- Who is assigned to what?
- What requires quality review?
- What is ready?
- What customers are being served?
- What training has been produced?
- What needs attention?
