# MMK DIGITAL FACTORY

## Internal Build v0.1

MMK Digital Factory is a digital production and entrepreneurship platform operated by MMK.

The Factory develops digital products and services, converts suitable production knowledge into training and repeatable workflows, and provides members with structured access to tools, learning and production opportunities.

Members can use Factory capabilities to:

- develop practical skills
- complete legitimate work
- earn according to agreed commercial arrangements
- build portfolios
- develop independent products
- develop independent businesses

MMK can generate revenue through:

- digital products
- digital services
- Factory access
- licensing
- subscriptions
- training
- legitimate commercial partnerships
- other approved commercial activities

## First Machine

The first actual Factory machine is the internal MMK Digital Factory Dashboard.

The dashboard manages the core pathway:

OPPORTUNITY → PRODUCT/SERVICE → MANUFACTURING → QUALITY → MEMBER → CUSTOMER/MARKET
