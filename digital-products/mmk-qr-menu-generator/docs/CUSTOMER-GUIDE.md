# MMK QR Menu Generator — Customer Guide

## How it works

Customers scan your QR code using their phone camera.

The QR code opens your digital menu.

## Updating your menu

The QR code does not need to be printed again when the authorized
menu PDF is replaced at the configured menu location.

## Important

Keep the QR code visible and protected from damage.

Only provide menu files that your business is authorized to distribute.
