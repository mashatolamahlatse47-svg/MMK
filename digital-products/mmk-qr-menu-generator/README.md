# MMK QR Menu Generator

MMK QR Menu Generator is a reusable digital-menu product for restaurants,
cafes, salons, takeaways, events and small businesses.

## What it does

1. Stores a digital PDF menu.
2. Creates a stable menu URL.
3. Generates a QR code pointing to that URL.
4. Customers scan the QR code with their phone.
5. The current PDF menu opens.
6. The business can replace/update the PDF without reprinting the QR code.
