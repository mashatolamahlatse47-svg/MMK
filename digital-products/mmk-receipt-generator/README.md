# MMK Receipt Generator

Version 1.1.0

## What It Does

MMK Receipt Generator is a simple browser-based tool for creating customer receipts.

It allows a small business to enter:

- Business name
- Phone / WhatsApp
- Receipt number
- Date
- Customer name
- Products or services
- Quantity
- Unit price
- Discount
- Payment method
- Payment reference

The system calculates the receipt total automatically.

## How To Use

1. Open the application.
2. Enter the business details.
3. Enter the receipt information.
4. Add products or services.
5. Enter quantity and price.
6. Choose the payment method.
7. Enter an optional payment reference.
8. Add a discount if required.
9. Select Generate Receipt.
10. Check the receipt.
11. Select Print / Save PDF.

## Example

5 Vetkoek × R10.00 = R50.00

2 Cold Drinks × R15.00 = R30.00

Subtotal = R80.00

Discount = R0.00

Total Paid = R80.00
