# MMK — WhatsApp Order Forms
## Small-business setup service

Help takeaways, cafés, caterers, bakeries and other small sellers receive
clearer orders through WhatsApp.

### Suggested introductory offer
- Basic setup: R250
- Branded menu and product setup: R350
- Optional menu updates: price agreed with the customer

These are proposed MMK prices, not verified market rates.

### Ask the customer for
1. Business name and logo (if available).
2. Approved WhatsApp number.
3. Product names and prices.
4. Collection/delivery rules.
5. Contact details and preferred colours.

### Before accepting payment
Demonstrate the complete order on a second phone. Confirm the menu is
available through the customer's actual link, the correct business receives
the WhatsApp message, and totals are correct.

Do not promise online synchronisation, secure accounts, payment processing,
or production hosting until those features are implemented and tested.
