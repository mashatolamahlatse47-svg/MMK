# MMK BUSINESS STARTER DESIGN PACK

Product ID: MMK-DESIGN-BUSINESS-STARTER
Version: 1.0.0
Category: Digital Asset
Type: Design Pack
Status: Development

## PURPOSE

A reusable collection of professional business design templates for
small businesses, entrepreneurs, creators and service providers.

The master design system must support multiple customer versions without
rebuilding the product from scratch.

## PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

MASTER
  ↓
GENERIC COMMERCIAL VERSION
  ↓
MMK-BRANDED VERSION
  ↓
CLIENT-CUSTOMIZED VERSION

## MASTER RULE

The master version is the reusable source.

Brand-specific information must not be hard-coded into the master.

Examples of replaceable fields:

- Business name
- Logo
- Owner name
- Phone number
- Email
- Address
- Website
- Social media handles
- Services
- Products
- Prices
- Promotional message
- Brand colors
- Brand fonts

## VERSION TYPES

### MASTER

Internal reusable source.

### GENERIC

Customer-ready version without MMK-specific branding.

### MMK

MMK-branded implementation derived from the master.

### CLIENT

Future customized implementation derived from the master.

## REUSABILITY

The design pack must be reusable for:

- Multiple businesses
- Multiple industries
- Multiple clients
- Multiple brand identities
- Future MMK products
- Future template bundles

Do not duplicate the entire product unnecessarily when a reusable
master can be adapted.
