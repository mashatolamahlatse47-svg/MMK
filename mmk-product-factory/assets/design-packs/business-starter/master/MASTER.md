# MMK BUSINESS STARTER DESIGN PACK
# MASTER DESIGN SYSTEM

Product ID: MMK-DESIGN-BUSINESS-STARTER
Master Version: 1.0.0
Asset Type: Reusable Design System
Status: Development

## 1. PURPOSE

This MASTER is the reusable design foundation for the
MMK Business Starter Design Pack.

It is designed to create professional business communication
materials for small businesses, entrepreneurs, creators and service providers.

The MASTER must be adaptable without rebuilding the entire
design system.

## 2. MASTER ARCHITECTURE

MASTER
  ↓
GENERIC COMMERCIAL VERSION
  ↓
MMK-BRANDED VERSION
  ↓
CLIENT-CUSTOMIZED VERSION

The MASTER contains structure, layout rules, typography rules,
spacing rules, reusable components and replaceable content fields.

Business-specific information must not be hard-coded into the MASTER.

## 3. DESIGN STYLE

Primary direction:

- Clean
- Professional
- Modern
- Simple
- Trustworthy
- Easy to read
- Easy to customize
- Commercially usable

Avoid unnecessary decoration.

Every element must serve a communication purpose.

## 5. DESIGN TOKENS

### Typography

Use a maximum of two font families.

Recommended structure:

- Primary font: headings and important information
- Secondary font: body text and supporting information

Maintain clear hierarchy between:

- Main heading
- Section heading
- Subheading
- Body text
- Small supporting text
- Call-to-action

### Spacing

Use consistent spacing between:

- Sections
- Headings
- Paragraphs
- Contact information
- Buttons
- Images
- Prices
- Product information

Avoid overcrowding.

### Layout

Layouts must support:

- Clear alignment
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Consistent margins
- Adequate whitespace
- Easy scanning
- Mobile readability where applicable
- Print readability where applicable
