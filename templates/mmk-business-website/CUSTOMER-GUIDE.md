# Customer Guide

## What This Template Is

A reusable website foundation that MMK can customize for a business.

## Information To Provide

- Business name
- Business description
- Logo
- Founder/owner name
- Services
- Products
- Phone number
- WhatsApp number
- Email address
- Location
- Business hours
- Social media links
- Hero image
- Gallery images

## Customer Website Sections

- Home
- About
- Services
- Products
- Gallery
- Projects / Portfolio
- Contact
- Footer

## Availability

Customer offerings should clearly identify whether something is:

- Available
- In Development
- Coming Soon

## Customization

MMK should customize the business content without changing the
underlying template unnecessarily.
